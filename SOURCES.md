## Caveats worth knowing

- **The four-phase picture is a simplification.** StatPearls describes two overlapping cycles: the ovary's (follicular, ovulation, luteal) and the uterus lining's (menstrual, proliferative, secretory). The follicular phase starts on Day 1, so the period happens inside it. The game's clock shows "Menstrual" for days 1 to 5 and "Follicular" for days 6 to 13, which is how Cleveland Clinic and most school materials teach it. The Unit 3 slide now says so.
- **Sources disagree on a few numbers.** The game uses ACOG's where they differ.
  - How often to change a pad: ACOG says at least every 4 to 8 hours; KidsHealth says every 3 to 4 hours.
  - How long until cycles are regular: ACOG says it may take 6 years or more; OWH and Cleveland Clinic say about 3 years.
  - Normal adult cycle length: Cleveland Clinic says 21 to 35 days; OWH and StatPearls say 24 to 38. The game only states the teen range (21 to 45, ACOG).
  - Average age of first period: ACOG says 12 to 13; OWH and Cleveland Clinic say 12; StatPearls gives a median of 12.4.
- **"More energy after your period" is weakly supported.** No source I read says energy rises in that week. Day 9's "Full Battery" theme is game design.
- **ACOG Committee Opinion 651:** I read the abstract only.
